Teaching has run alongside everything else I do for as long as I've
been doing anything worth teaching.

- **National-team ultimate frisbee coach** ([2011–2017]) — coached
  the French national team through several international campaigns,
  and co-wrote a book on team concepts and coaching frameworks.
- **Music teaching** ([2022 → today]) — voice, guitar, bass, and general
  musicianship, from beginners to gigging players. Private lessons,
  small-group workshops, and one-off masterclasses.
- **Developer mentoring** — carried an apprentice through his first
  production project at ITSF, and generally treat every codebase I
  work in as somebody's future learning material.
- **Ad-hoc facilitation** — the miscellaneous rest: bandmates I've
  helped through their own writing, students I've walked through a
  first live gig, colleagues I've unstuck on a debugging problem.

The through-line is the same across all of them: I'm interested in
the moment where somebody stops needing me. That's the moment I'm
actually paid for.
