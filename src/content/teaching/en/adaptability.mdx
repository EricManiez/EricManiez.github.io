Meet people where they are. Get them out of their comfort zone. And
flex with them when the ground shifts under your feet.

**Intellectual traction** — the moment the student's brain actually
grabs onto something — is at the core of what I try to create. That
demands flexibility: from me, from the plan, from the exercise, from
the tempo. A lesson plan that ignores what's happening in front of
you isn't teaching. It's a monologue.
