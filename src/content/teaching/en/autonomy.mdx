Teaching, at its best, is about helping people build **the skill, the
confidence, and the power to decide for themselves**.

The measure of a good lesson isn't how much I explained or how
smoothly I demonstrated. It's whether the student walks out with more
ability to figure the next thing out on their own — and knows it.
