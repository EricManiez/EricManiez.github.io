There's something beautiful in buckling down and getting serious about
your craft. And there's no reason that should mean stiffening up.

Serious **and** laid back — not back-and-forth between the two, both
at the same time. The best musical moments I remember, as a student
and as a teacher, held both at once: full presence, full attention,
zero self-importance.
