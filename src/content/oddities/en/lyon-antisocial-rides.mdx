recurring humour piece that celebrates the solitary nature of the
sport. Every ride is a "group ride" of exactly one, immortalised on

A gentle poke at the tribal, always-in-a-pack vibe of endurance sport
culture — and a reminder that the ride is still the ride, party or no.
