I've always had a lot to say while watching a sporting event, and that
first became a professional endeavour when I commented the finals of the
2010 edition of the legendary **Paganello** beach ultimate tournament.

In 2021, when my company inexplicably became engrossed in a darts
obsession and organised a tournament, I forewent participating in order
to put together a commented livestream of the event. I learned OBS, set
up a three-camera rig, wired a soundboard, and leaned on my existing
audio and MIDI knowledge to complete the setup — and both commented and
directed the livestream.
