A sign, two chairs, and a chess clock.

Will you press the button and see what a deep conversation with a
stranger is like? Will you sidestep into the intimacy and vulnerability
of the moment when it serendipitously stumbles into your packed routine?

An experiment in creating small, contained spaces where strangers can
opt in to something more than the usual public-space transaction.
