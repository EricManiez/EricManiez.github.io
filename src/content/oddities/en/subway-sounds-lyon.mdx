After watching this video, I knew my city needed its own train melodies.
What better place to start than the bite-sized, four-station **C Line**?

The scope is contained enough to be an achievable first pass — four
short, distinct musical motifs, one per station, each with a family
resemblance to the others. From there, the harder question: how would
you scale the idea across the rest of the network?
