Leave The Biker was my leading creative project for five years — a power
pop outfit built around big choruses, jangly guitars, and songs that ask to
be sung along with. I wrote the material, sang lead, and handled the
guitar chair.

The band ran from 2019 to 2024, wrapping with **HEAVIER ROMANCE** — an
album that felt like the right note to end on.
