Emma writes songs that live in the space between confession and lullaby —
folk pop with room to breathe, built around a voice that never pushes when
it doesn't need to.

On stage and in the studio I'm the swiss-army knife: bass to anchor the low
end, backup vocals to widen the harmony, lap steel to blur the edges when a
track needs a touch of sky. My job is to serve the song and disappear when
that's the right move.
