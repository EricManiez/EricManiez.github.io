Ultimate has been the sport of my life. Played across two continents
and many countries, at every level from Sunday-league to national team.

**As a player.** Led a bottom-dwelling Lyon side to a 6th-place national
finish in 2011, then — in 2015–16 — captained the **French men's
national team** to a 3rd-place finish at the European Championships.

**As a coach.** From 2011 to 2017 I was head coach of the **French U20
women's team**. In that stretch we brought home two European medals
and finished top-5 at a World Championship.

**On the page.** Co-authored a reference book on team fundamentals and
tactical variations — the PDF is embedded below.
