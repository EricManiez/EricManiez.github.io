Watched a lot of basketball — probably too much — and one day in 2017
finally acted on it: joined a competitive club and started learning
to play from scratch, as an adult, alongside people who'd been at it
since childhood.

Started out as a **defensive force** — the easier path into a game
when your instincts aren't yet the ones honed by years of pickup. Over
time, some **offensive finesse** followed: shot selection, footwork,
reading the game before the ball arrives. Still the fun part.
