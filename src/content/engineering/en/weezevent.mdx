Started my professional career at Weezevent, a European event-ticketing
platform, on a fullstack team of about fifteen with direct working lines
to both the Lead Dev and the CTO.

The headline chapter was the platform's **internationalisation** — with
its multi-timezone dimension. Any ticketing product that grows
international eventually has to reckon with time as a first-class citizen:
event windows, sales cutoffs, refunds, reporting — all of them look
different depending on where the event, the operator, and the buyer sit.
The work demanded both technical rigor and a fine-grained read of how
event operators actually behave across regions.
