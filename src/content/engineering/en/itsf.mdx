Joined ITSF, the IT arm of a telecom holding, to build and maintain
custom software for their client **Monaco Telecom**. The mission
started on the accounting stack — but the shape of the work stretched
well beyond a pure developer role: daily client contact, regular
on-site interventions, and the responsibility of translating fuzzy
operational needs into concrete deliverables.

The most defining project of the period was a bespoke **automation
solution**, entirely new, that I designed and built from a client-side
need. I ran it in partnership with an apprentice I was mentoring —
which meant carrying two demands at once: ship something the client
could rely on, and use the same project to grow another developer.
That dual mandate — deliver and teach — is where the "product builder"
shape of my practice really settled in.
