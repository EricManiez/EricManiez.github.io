When I pivoted to full-time musical pedagogy in 2022, I didn't want to
let the coder's practice atrophy. **MuseScore** — the leading
open-source music notation software — was the natural place to keep
that practice honest: a large, mature C++ codebase with a healthy
community of contributors and a domain I care about deeply.

The contributions are small and careful by design. The point is less to
ship dramatic features than to stay fluent — reading unfamiliar code,
following review conventions, working inside a team culture that isn't
mine to shape.
