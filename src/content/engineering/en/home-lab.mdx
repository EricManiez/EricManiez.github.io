The residuum: everything I've built, wired, or scripted for its own
sake.

- **Home DNS stack** — a Pi-hole + Unbound setup that keeps my network
  sane and gave me an excuse to relearn zone files.
- **Back-office automation** — a set of Excel workflows that runs the
  administrative machinery of a small association without needing a
  human in the loop for the boring parts.
- **Physical prototyping** — Arduino for anything that needs a sensor
  or a servo; 3D printing for anything the shop doesn't sell.
- **AI-in-the-loop dev** — comfortable with LLM CLIs (**Claude**,
  **Claude Code**) and running smaller models locally via **MLX** for
  work that shouldn't leave the machine.

None of this pays. All of it keeps the fingers, the network intuition,
and the taste for building things alive.
